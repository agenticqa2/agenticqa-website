Who we are

An AI and quality engineering company.

Agentic QA Technologies LLP (LLPIN ACV-7177, registered in Puducherry, India) works where AI systems meet software quality: building, testing and evaluating AI-powered software, and helping people and teams do the same.

AI-native quality engineering

Playwright and API automation with AI-assisted triage, CI/CD integration and test strategy for modern web products.

Custom AI agents

Task-specific agents connected to company data and tools through APIs and MCP servers, with human checkpoints where they matter.

LLM & agent evaluation

Evaluation suites (DeepEval, Ragas, LangSmith) that measure accuracy, safety and regressions in LLM and RAG features.

Automation consulting

Mapping manual workflows, finding high-value automation candidates and delivering a practical roadmap.

Skill programmes

Structured, hands-on programmes in AI engineering, Playwright + AI and NEET preparation, each run on its own site.

In-house tooling

Applied AI products built and maintained by our own team, used as reference implementations.

Our aims

Deliver reliable AI and QA outcomes for business clients, and make engineers job-ready for the AI era by working the way real teams practise: live, hands-on and project-driven.

Our goals

Build durable in-house tooling and programmes; serve companies with consulting and custom agents; and support learners through clear pathways — AI engineering, Playwright + AI, and NEET preparation through CatalystAI.

Graduates of our programmes receive an Industry Internship Completion Certificate from Agentic QA Technologies. Proof of internship-style delivery work with the company: projects, reviews and a defined scope — not a government or university diploma.
