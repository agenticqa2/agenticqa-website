What technologies do you cover?

We specialize in AI-native QA automation including Playwright, TypeScript, Python, LangChain, RAG systems, Agentic AI, MCP Servers, and LLM integration for testing.

Yes! We provide customized corporate training programs for teams looking to upskill in AI-native test automation. Contact us for a tailored curriculum.

Our 28-day AI/ML internship includes 1:1 mentorship, hands-on project work, certificate of completion, and flexible scheduling. Visit our Students page for details.

Yes! We now serve clients in the European Union with timezone-friendly scheduling and GDPR-compliant services. We accept both INR and EUR payments.
