Frequently Asked Questions

What is AgenticQA?

AgenticQA is an AI-native QA automation platform that combines LLMs, RAG, and Agentic AI with Playwright for intelligent test automation. It's designed to make test automation more adaptive and maintainable.

Who is this for?

This platform and its projects are designed for QA engineers, automation engineers, final year students (BSc, MSc, BE, MCA), and professionals looking to upskill in AI-powered testing.

Are the projects production-ready?

Yes! Each project is built with production-quality code, proper error handling, and industry best practices. They're designed to be interview-ready and demonstrate real-world skills.

Do I need expensive AI APIs?

No! All projects support free local LLMs via Ollama as the primary option. OpenAI/Claude integration is available as an optional paid tier for enhanced performance.

When will the AI QA Bootcamp launch?

The bootcamp with 6 additional projects is currently in development. Join the waitlist via the contact form to get early access notifications.

Can I use these projects for my college submission?

Yes! The projects are specifically designed to be valuable for final year students. Each project includes documentation, architecture diagrams, and presentation materials.
